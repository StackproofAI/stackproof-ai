COMMON QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Understand what an assessment includes before you decide to start a conversation.

What happens during the complimentary assessment?+

We discuss your current process, tools, and goals. We then identify up to three potential automation opportunities and outline possible next steps. The assessment is advisory and does not include implementation.

Do we need to replace our existing CRM or software?+

Not necessarily. The first step is to understand the systems you already use and determine whether they can support a better workflow.

Does STACKPROOF AI build and implement workflows?+

Implementation can be scoped separately after the requirements, integrations, risks, and success measures are understood.

Is the initial assessment free?+

Yes. The initial opportunity assessment is complimentary and carries no obligation to purchase implementation services.
