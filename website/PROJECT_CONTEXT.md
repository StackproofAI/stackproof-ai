# STACKPROOF AI Website — Project Context

## Purpose
Public B2B website for STACKPROOF AI, an AI automation and business operations consultancy. The website's primary conversion is a complimentary, no-obligation 15-minute introduction and advisory automation opportunity assessment.

## Audience
Owners, operators, and decision-makers at growing businesses. Public positioning remains industry-neutral rather than focused on one vertical.
